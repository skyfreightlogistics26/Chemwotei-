About Sky Freight

Local understanding. Business-minded logistics.

Based in Eldoret, Kenya, Sky Freight & Logistics is focused on making freight coordination more straightforward for businesses that need to move goods. Our brokerage-led model connects customers with third-party transport providers rather than claiming to operate a company-owned fleet.

  • Transport options matched to cargo and route requirements
  • Clear quotation and service discussions before confirmation
  • Coordination of loading, transit communication and delivery
  • Document and insurance checks discussed for each shipment
